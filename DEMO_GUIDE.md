# CampusPilot AI: demo guide for judges (3–5 minutes)

> All student, course and job data is **fictional** (student: "Alex Morgan", Bay Atlantic University demo profile). CampusPilot is not affiliated with or endorsed by BAU. LearningBAU sync is **simulated**. Reminders are **in-app only**: they appear while CampusPilot is open and are never sent as push or email notifications. Nothing here is legal or immigration advice.

## Before you present (2 minutes)

1. Start the app:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Then open http://localhost:3000 in a laptop-size window.
2. Click **Reset demo data** at the bottom of the sidebar and confirm. This restores:
   - fictional assignments, with due dates set relative to *now*, so the "overdue", "< 2h" and "< 24h" states always show
   - the draft graduation plan with one intentional **prerequisite conflict**: SWE 410 Advanced Software Engineering is scheduled in the same term as its prerequisite CS 102 Programming II
3. Optional live AI: if `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set in `.env.local` (free tier, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`), the sidebar shows **Gemini connected**. Each Assistant question uses **one** free-tier request, and repeated questions are cached for 10 minutes. Without a key, every feature still works and is labeled **Rule-based**.

## The demo flow

### 1. Dashboard: "where do I stand?" (30 s)
- Three live numbers at the top: tasks done this week, degree progress with projected graduation, and the next deadline.
- **Today's focus** combines alerts from all modules: an overdue assignment, a graduation risk, an immigration milestone, internship matches.
- Line: *"This isn't a chatbot waiting for questions. It notices problems across your whole student life."*

### 2. Smart Study Board: the original feature (60 s)
- Open **Smart Study Board**. Show the week laid out by day, pastel sticky notes, and urgency badges.
- Click the **✓** on a note: it turns **gray with a green check (no strikethrough)**, confetti and balloons appear, and weekly progress goes up.
- Use **← / →** to show week navigation, then **Back to this week**.
- Click **Dashboard**: the progress number already reflects the completed task (shared state, nothing re-entered).

### 3. Graduation Planner: the visible conflict (60 s)
- The **Graduation timeline** shows Spring 2027 marked **prereq conflict**.
- In **Graduation risk warnings**, read "Why this delays you". SWE 410 can't start until CS 102 is done, and it holds up SWE 420, SWE 490 and SWE 491.
- Click **Move SWE 410 to Summer 2027 (earliest valid term)**. The conflict disappears, the timeline updates, and the dashboard alert clears.
- Scroll to **Planning scenarios**: Standard → **May 2029** vs Accelerated → **May 2028**, each with a "Why?" box computed by the scheduler.
- Line: *"Every date and credit here is calculated deterministically. AI never invents them."*

### 4. CampusPilot Assistant: the connected brain (60 s)
- Click **Ask CampusPilot** (Graduation header) or use a suggested chip: *"Can I graduate by May 2028 and still complete a summer internship?"*
- Point out the answer label (**Generated by Gemini · verified** or **Rule-based**), the module chips (Graduation · International · Career), **deterministic facts used**, and the collapsible **official sources**.
- Click **Add task: ask DSO about summer CPT timing**. A **confirmation panel** appears and nothing changes until **Confirm & add** is clicked. Then open the Study Board and show the new note (a confirmed action updating another module).

### 5. Career Navigator (30–45 s)
- Search "python" or pick a field chip. Show the match % and click **why?** for the score breakdown.
- Open **Fit analysis, CPT/OPT & tasks**: strengths and gaps, skill plan tied to planned courses (e.g. CS 360 in Spring 2028), and work-authorization guidance that **never assumes** an employer accepts CPT/OPT.
- Listings are labeled **Fictional demo listing**. Real postings appear only if public job boards are configured, and they're always labeled live, with work authorization "Unknown".

### 6. International Student Advisor (optional, 20 s)
- "What CampusPilot can do" vs "What only your DSO or USCIS can decide".
- Timeline dates come from cited USCIS timing rules (OPT filing window: 90 days before to 60 days after the program end date).
- **Draft DSO email** opens an editable draft. Nothing is sent.

## Honest answers to likely judge questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is the AI real? | Yes when a key is set: Gemini `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` on the free tier, called only from the server. It rephrases deterministic facts, and every answer is validated (numbers, citations, no eligibility decisions) or replaced by the rule-based answer, with the reason shown. |
| Does it send notifications? | No. Reminders are in-app only, while the app is open. |
| Is it connected to LearningBAU? | No. That integration is simulated, and the data is fictional. |
| Are the jobs real? | The 8 sample listings are fictional. The optional live layer reads public Greenhouse/Lever boards and never infers sponsorship. |
| Where is data stored? | In the browser (localStorage). There are no accounts or servers storing student data. |

## If something goes wrong

- **AI answer labeled rule-based:** that's expected without a key, after the free quota is used, or when verification rejects an answer. The demo still works.
- **Data looks different from this guide:** click **Reset demo data**.
- **Port 3000 busy:** stop the other process, or run `npm run dev -- -p 3001`.
